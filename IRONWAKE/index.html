How to fight

Helm

  • Mouse look around your ship (click the view to capture it; arrow keys also work)
  • W S throttle · A D rudder
  • Shift boost (hold)
  • N plot a course · J warp drive
  • F dock when close and slow
  • M sector chart · Esc pause

Gunnery

  • Look to port or starboard to pick a broadside
  • Hold Left mouse or Space to steady the guns; the fan narrows
  • Release to fire the whole battery
  • Lead a moving ship: aim at the red marker
  • Turrets fire on their own at anything hostile in reach
  • Q torpedo at the selected ship

Survival

  • Right mouse or E raise the deflector: it soaks a salvo, then must recharge
  • You have four shield screens. Turn a fresh one toward the enemy
  • R nearest hostile · T next ship
  • Broadsides crack small rocks into ore; cargo is tractored in automatically

Paused

Your game is saved each time you dock. Quitting at sea returns you to your last port.

Ship lost

She goes down with her colours flying.
