How to fly

Flight

  • Mouse steer — the ship turns toward the cursor
  • W S or wheel — throttle up / down
  • A D strafe · X brake
  • Shift afterburner (hold)
  • C cruise drive — fast travel, no guns
  • Z cut engines and drift
  • Space free the cursor · arrows also steer

Combat

  • Left mouse fire guns at the cursor
  • Right mouse select enemy under cursor
  • Q launch missile
  • R nearest hostile · T next ship
  • Y target what the cursor is on
  • H hull patch · J shield cell
  • B tractor in floating cargo
  • Aim at the red lead marker to hit a moving ship

Travel

  • N next place · M navigation map
  • F dock / jump / ride — flies you there first
  • G autopilot to target
  • Slingway rings fling you across a system
  • Fold gates jump between systems
  • Esc or P pause

Paused

Your game is saved each time you dock. Quitting in flight returns you to your last port.

Ship lost

Your hull breaks up in silence.
